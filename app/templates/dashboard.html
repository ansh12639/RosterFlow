How scheduling works

  1. Brand/process calendars define demand (minimum staff per day, holidays, sale days).
  2. Approved leave, comp-offs and fixed offs are hard constraints.
  3. Weekly offs are placed where they don't break coverage; preferences yield to client coverage.
  4. Remaining gaps are filled by designated backups, or flagged with ranked cover suggestions.
  5. Every edit is re-validated; errors block publishing unless acknowledged.
